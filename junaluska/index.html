Junaluska’s Journey: A Cherokee’s Trail of Tears

A Story of Loyalty, Regret, and Resilience

by Thomas Stonis

ShadowDog Digital Studios · 2024

Junaluska was a real Cherokee leader — the man who once saved Andrew Jackson’s life in battle, and who then watched that same president drive his people west. This short novel follows him along the Trail of Tears, telling the removal not as a date in a textbook but as one man’s story of loyalty betrayed and a people’s endurance.

There’s a short classroom activity to go with it — read the book, then tell the real history from what the author imagined.
